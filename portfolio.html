Project Overview

Making Sustainability
Addictive

⚡ The Challenge

78% of young adults want to adopt eco-friendly habits, but lack motivation and consistent feedback to maintain them. Existing apps are either too preachy, boring, or fail to create lasting behavior change.

🌿 The Solution

EcoLoop transforms environmental actions into a game-like experience with XP rewards, streak mechanics, social leaderboards, and real-world impact tracking — making sustainability as addictive as Duolingo.

Research Phase

Methodology

A mixed-methods approach combining quantitative surveys with qualitative interviews to uncover the gap between sustainability intent and action.

Q: What stops you from being more eco-friendly? "I don't see the difference my small actions make — it all feels pointless without feedback."
Q: Which apps keep you coming back daily? "Duolingo, because of the streak. I can't break it. If eco-habits had that pressure, I'd definitely do more."
Q: How do you track your sustainability efforts? "I don't, honestly. Nothing makes it easy or fun enough to bother."
01

Secondary Research

  • Market analysis: $42.3B sustainability app market, 23% YoY growth
  • Competitive audit: Forest, Duolingo, Habitica feature comparison
  • Literature review: Gamification psychology & behavior change models
02

Primary Research

  • 8 semi-structured interviews (ages 22–35, urban residents)
  • Online survey: 45 responses on eco-habits & app usage
  • Affinity mapping to identify pain points & opportunities

Design Token Architecture

A unified token system ensures visual consistency across platforms while respecting native design patterns for iOS (Human Interface) and Android (Material You).

Adaptive Component Library

Components adapt to platform conventions — bottom sheets on iOS, bottom navigation on Android — while maintaining EcoLoop's signature visual identity.

Shared Interaction Patterns

Core gamification mechanics (streaks, XP, leaderboards) remain identical across platforms for a seamless cross-device experience.
